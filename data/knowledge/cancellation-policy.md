# Order Cancellation Policy

**Solara Home Electronics**
Last updated: March 1, 2026

## Cancelling an Order

Because our fulfillment centers begin processing orders quickly, cancellation windows are limited. Whether you can cancel depends on your order's current status.

| Order Status | Can You Cancel? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Order Received (not yet processing) | Yes, instantly | My Orders > Cancel Order |
| Processing (being picked/packed) | Sometimes — request via Support, not guaranteed | Contact Support immediately |
| Shipped | No — refuse delivery or return per our [Return Policy](return-policy.md) | N/A |
| Pre-order / Backorder | Yes, any time before the item ships | My Orders > Cancel Order |

Most standard orders can be cancelled free of charge within **60 minutes** of placing the order, after which they typically move to "Processing" and may no longer be cancellable through self-service.

## How to Cancel

1. Go to **My Orders** in your Solara account.
2. Select the order you want to cancel.
3. Click **Cancel Order**. If the option isn't available, the order has already entered processing.
4. If self-service cancellation isn't available, contact Customer Support at support@solarahome.com or 1-888-765-6272 immediately — we will attempt to intercept the order but cannot guarantee success.

## Refunds for Cancelled Orders

Successfully cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. No cancellation fee applies to standard orders.

## Cancelling Subscription Services (Solara+ and Consumables)

Solara+ membership and auto-replenishment consumable subscriptions (e.g., air filter refills) can be cancelled at any time:

1. Go to **Account > Subscriptions**.
2. Select the subscription and click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle; you retain access/benefits until that date.
4. No partial refunds are issued for the remainder of an active billing period, except where required by law.

## Cancelling Installation or Assembly Services

For scheduled in-home installation (smart home setup, appliance installation):

- Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before your appointment window.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance, or a missed appointment, incur a $49 service fee.

## Custom and Made-to-Order Items

Products labeled "Built to Order" (e.g., custom-panel Solara Chill refrigerators) can only be cancelled within 4 hours of order placement, after which manufacturing begins and the order is final.

## Cancellations Initiated by Solara

We reserve the right to cancel an order, in whole or in part, for reasons including but not limited to: item unavailability, pricing errors, suspected fraud, or shipping restrictions to your region. If we cancel your order, you will be notified by email and refunded in full within 5 business days with no action required on your part.
