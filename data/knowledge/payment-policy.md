# Payment Policy

**Solara Home Electronics**
Last updated: March 1, 2026

## Accepted Payment Methods

- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay (on mobile and supported browsers)
- Solara Store Credit and Gift Cards
- Solara Pay Later, powered by Affirm (installment financing, subject to credit approval)
- Corporate purchase orders (for verified Solara Business accounts only)

We do not accept personal checks, money orders, or cryptocurrency at this time.

## When You're Charged

- **In-stock items:** Your payment method is authorized at checkout and charged when the order ships.
- **Pre-orders and backorders:** A temporary authorization hold is placed at checkout; the actual charge occurs when the item ships. You may cancel any time before shipment at no cost.
- **Split shipments:** If your order ships in multiple packages, you are charged proportionally as each package ships, not the full amount up front.
- **Solara Pay Later:** Affirm performs a soft credit check and, if approved, pays Solara directly; you repay Affirm per your chosen installment plan (typically 3, 6, or 12 months). APR ranges from 0%–29.99% based on creditworthiness, as disclosed by Affirm at checkout.

## Sales Tax

Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping address and applicable state/local tax law. Tax-exempt organizations may submit exemption documentation to tax-exempt@solarahome.com prior to placing an order for review.

## Failed or Declined Payments

If a payment fails at checkout, common causes include:

- Insufficient funds or credit limit reached
- Billing address mismatch with the card issuer's records
- Card expired or flagged by your bank for a new/unusual merchant
- Incorrect CVV or card number entry

See [Troubleshooting: Payment Failed](../troubleshooting/payment-failed.md) for step-by-step resolution.

## Authorization Holds

When you place an order, your bank may show a temporary authorization hold that is separate from the actual charge. Holds typically fall off within 3–7 business days if an order is cancelled or a temporary auth is not captured, though exact timing depends on your bank's policies, not Solara's.

## Promo Codes and Gift Cards

- Only one promo code may be applied per order unless otherwise stated in the specific promotion's terms.
- Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively to completed orders except within 24 hours of purchase, and only by contacting Support.
- Gift cards do not expire and carry no maintenance fees. Balances can be checked at www.solarahome.com/giftcard-balance.
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash except where required by state law.

## Currency

All prices on www.solarahome.com are listed in USD for orders shipping within the United States, and CAD for orders shipping within Canada (solarahome.ca). International cards will be charged in the listed currency; your bank may apply its own conversion rate and foreign transaction fee.

## Security

Solara is PCI-DSS compliant. We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers; payment processing is handled by our certified payment partners (Stripe and PayPal). For more information, see our Privacy Policy at www.solarahome.com/privacy.

## Billing Disputes

If you believe you've been charged in error, contact billing@solarahome.com with your order number before initiating a chargeback with your bank — most billing issues can be resolved within 1–2 business days directly with our team.
