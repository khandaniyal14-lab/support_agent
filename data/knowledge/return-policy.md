# Return Policy

**Solara Home Electronics**
Last updated: March 1, 2026

## Standard Return Window

Most items purchased from Solara Home Electronics may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange, provided the item meets the conditions below.

| Product Category | Return Window |
|---|---|
| Smart home devices (hubs, plugs, sensors) | 30 days |
| Smart lighting | 30 days |
| Robot vacuums & floor care | 30 days |
| Audio (speakers, soundbars) | 30 days |
| Wearables & personal care electronics | 15 days (must be unopened/unused for hygiene reasons) |
| Major appliances (Solara Chill, Solara Cook lines) | 30 days, free return pickup available |
| Open-box / Refurbished items | 15 days |
| Gift cards, software licenses, extended warranties | Not returnable |

## Condition Requirements

To be eligible for a full refund, returned items must be:

- In original condition, unused (except for reasonable inspection/testing), and free of damage not caused by Solara.
- In the original packaging with all accessories, cables, manuals, and included promotional items.
- Accompanied by the original proof of purchase (order number or receipt).

Items that do not meet these conditions may still be accepted at Solara's discretion, subject to a restocking fee of up to 20%.

## How to Start a Return

1. Log in to your account and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Return or Replace Item**.
3. Choose a reason for the return and whether you'd like a refund, exchange, or store credit.
4. Print the prepaid shipping label (free for defective, damaged, or mis-shipped items; a $6.99 label fee applies to other returns and is deducted from your refund, unless you use your own shipping method).
5. Drop the package off at any authorized carrier location within 14 days of generating the label.

Don't have an account or ordered as a guest? Contact support@solarahome.com with your order number and email used at checkout.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order. Once your returned item is scanned by the carrier, we ship the replacement immediately rather than waiting for warehouse receipt, provided the item is in stock.

## Large Appliance Returns

For Solara Chill (refrigeration) and Solara Cook (ranges/ovens) products delivered via freight carrier:

- Contact Support to schedule a free return pickup — do not ship large appliances via standard mail.
- Items must be returned in the original crate/packaging whenever possible.
- A technician-verified inspection may be required prior to pickup for units that have been installed.

## Return Shipping Costs

| Reason for Return | Who Pays Shipping |
|---|---|
| Defective, damaged, or incorrect item | Solara (free prepaid label) |
| Changed your mind / no longer needed | Customer ($6.99 flat-rate label, or use your own carrier) |
| Wrong size/model ordered by mistake | Customer |

## International Returns

Orders shipped outside the United States and Canada must be returned via a trackable, insured shipping method at the customer's expense. Solara is not responsible for items lost in return transit without tracking. Refunds for international orders exclude original customs duties and import taxes, which are non-refundable.

## Non-Returnable Items

- Gift cards and Solara Store Credit
- Downloadable software, activated firmware licenses, and extended warranty plans
- Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout
- Personal care items (e.g., in-ear audio devices) once the hygiene seal is broken
