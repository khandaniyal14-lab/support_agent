# Troubleshooting: Payment Failed

**Solara Home Electronics**

If your payment was declined or an error occurred at checkout, work through the steps below in order.

## Step 1: Check the Basics

- Confirm your card number, expiration date, and CVV were entered correctly.
- Confirm the billing address matches exactly what's on file with your card issuer (including apartment/unit number and ZIP code).
- Confirm your card hasn't expired.
- Confirm you have sufficient available funds or credit, including for the estimated tax and shipping shown at checkout.

## Step 2: Try an Alternate Payment Method

If the issue persists, try:

- A different card
- PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay
- Solara Pay Later (Affirm), if your order is $50 or more

This helps determine whether the issue is with your specific card or a broader checkout problem.

## Step 3: Contact Your Bank

Banks sometimes decline first-time or high-value transactions from new merchants as a fraud-prevention measure, even when your account is in good standing. Call the number on the back of your card and ask them to authorize a transaction from "Solara Home Electronics." They can typically clear this in a few minutes.

## Step 4: Clear Checkout Blockers

- Disable ad blockers or privacy browser extensions, which can occasionally interfere with our payment form.
- Try a different browser or device, or the Solara mobile app.
- Clear your browser cache and cookies, or use a private/incognito window.

## Step 5: Check for a Duplicate Charge

If you attempted payment multiple times, check your bank statement for pending authorization holds — these are not actual charges and typically fall off within 3–7 business days. Only a completed order confirmation email means you were successfully charged.

## Common Error Messages

| Error | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Card declined" | Bank declined the transaction | Contact your bank, or use another payment method |
| "Address verification failed" | Billing address doesn't match card issuer's records | Re-enter the exact address on your card statement |
| "Payment processing error" | Temporary issue with payment gateway | Wait a few minutes and retry, or try a different browser |
| "This payment method is not supported for your region" | Card issuer or type not supported for shipping destination | Try PayPal or a different card |
