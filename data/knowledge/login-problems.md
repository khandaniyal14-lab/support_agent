# Troubleshooting: Login Problems

**Solara Home Electronics**

## "Incorrect email or password"

1. Double-check for typos, extra spaces, and that Caps Lock is off.
2. Confirm you're using the email address the account was created with — customers sometimes have separate personal and Solara+ business accounts under different addresses.
3. Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page to reset it. Reset links are valid for 60 minutes and sent to the email on file.
4. If you signed up using "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple," you won't have a Solara password — use that same social login button instead of entering a password.

## Not Receiving the Password Reset Email

- Check spam, junk, and promotions folders.
- Confirm the email address you're entering matches your account exactly (check for typos or an old/unused email).
- Add no-reply@solarahome.com to your contacts/safe senders list and request the reset again.
- Wait at least 10 minutes — during high-traffic periods, delivery can occasionally be delayed.
- Still nothing after 30 minutes? Contact Support to verify the email on file for your account.

## Account Temporarily Locked

Your account locks for 30 minutes after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts, as a security precaution. You can:

- Wait 30 minutes and try again, or
- Use **Forgot password?** to reset immediately and unlock the account, or
- Contact Support at support@solarahome.com for manual verification and unlock if you can't wait.

## "This account has been flagged for review"

This message appears when we detect unusual activity (e.g., sign-in attempts from an unfamiliar location combined with account or order changes). To resolve:

1. Check your email for a security verification message from security@solarahome.com and follow the instructions.
2. If you don't receive it within 15 minutes, contact Support — you may be asked to verify your identity using your order history or ID.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Issues

- **Not receiving the 2FA code by SMS:** Confirm your phone number under **Account > Security** is current; try requesting the code again after 60 seconds, or switch to email-based 2FA.
- **Lost access to your 2FA device:** Use one of the backup codes provided when you enabled 2FA, or contact Support for identity verification to regain access.

## Login Works on Website but Not the Mobile App (or Vice Versa)

- Confirm the app is updated to the latest version (App Store / Google Play).
- Log out and back in on the app.
- Uninstall and reinstall the app if the issue persists — your account data is stored server-side, not on the device, so no data is lost.

## Social Login (Google/Apple) Not Working

- Make sure you're signed in to the correct Google/Apple account on your device.
- Check that Solara has permission to access basic profile info in your Google/Apple account settings.
- If your Solara account was originally created with an email/password and you're now trying social login with the same email, link the accounts under **Account > Security > Linked Accounts**, or contact Support to link them for you.
