# FAQ: Accounts

**Solara Home Electronics**

**Q: Do I need an account to place an order?**
A: No, guest checkout is available. However, creating a free account lets you track orders, manage returns, save addresses/payment methods, and access Solara+ member pricing where applicable.

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?**. Enter the email associated with your account, and we'll send a reset link valid for 60 minutes. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or see [Login Problems troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/login-problems.md).

**Q: Can I merge my guest order into my new account?**
A: Yes. Create your account using the same email address used at checkout, then go to **My Orders > Link Previous Orders** and enter your order number — it will automatically appear in your order history.

**Q: How do I update my email address or password?**
A: Log in and go to **Account > Profile Settings**. Email changes require you to confirm the new address via a verification link before the change takes effect.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to **Account > Privacy Settings > Delete Account**, or email privacy@solarahome.com. Per our Privacy Policy, we'll delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we're required to retain for tax, warranty, or legal purposes.

**Q: What is Solara+ membership?**
A: Solara+ is our optional paid membership ($79/year or $8.99/month) offering free expedited shipping, early access to sales, member-exclusive pricing, and extended return windows (45 days instead of 30). Manage or cancel anytime under **Account > Subscriptions**.

**Q: Can I have multiple shipping addresses saved?**
A: Yes, you can save unlimited addresses under **Account > Address Book** and choose any of them at checkout.

**Q: Why was my account locked?**
A: Accounts are temporarily locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts, or automatically flagged for review if we detect unusual activity (e.g., sign-in from a new device combined with a large order). See [Login Problems troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/login-problems.md) or contact support@solarahome.com to unlock it.

**Q: How do I connect my Solara account to my smart home devices?**
A: Download the Solara Home app (iOS/Android), sign in with your Solara account, and follow the in-app setup wizard to add devices. Your purchase account and your smart-home app account use the same login.

**Q: Is my personal information sold to third parties?**
A: No. Solara does not sell personal information. Details on data collection, use, and your rights are available in our Privacy Policy at www.solarahome.com/privacy.
