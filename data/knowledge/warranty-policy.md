# Warranty Policy

**Solara Home Electronics**
Last updated: March 1, 2026

## Standard Manufacturer Warranty

All new Solara-branded products purchased from www.solarahome.com or an authorized Solara retailer include the following limited warranty against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use:

| Product Category | Standard Warranty |
|---|---|
| Smart home hubs, plugs, sensors, switches | 2 years |
| Smart lighting | 2 years |
| Robot vacuums & floor care | 1 year (battery: 6 months) |
| Audio (speakers, soundbars) | 1 year |
| Wearables | 1 year |
| Solara Chill (refrigeration) | 2 years parts & labor; 5 years on compressor (parts only) |
| Solara Cook (ranges, ovens, cooktops) | 2 years parts & labor |
| Batteries (standalone/replacement) | 6 months |
| Refurbished / Open-box items | 90 days |
| Accessories & cables | 90 days |

Warranty coverage begins on the delivery date shown in your order confirmation, not the manufacture date.

## What's Covered

- Defects in materials or workmanship under normal residential use
- Premature battery failure below 80% of rated capacity within the battery warranty period
- Firmware-related hardware failures caused by an official Solara update

## What's Not Covered

- Normal wear and tear (e.g., scuffing, fading, brush wear on vacuum parts)
- Accidental damage, drops, liquid damage not specifically rated (see IP rating on your product's spec sheet), or misuse
- Damage from unauthorized repairs, modifications, or use of non-Solara replacement parts
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Products purchased from unauthorized third-party sellers (including most third-party marketplace listings not fulfilled by Solara)
- Commercial or non-residential use, unless covered under a Solara Business warranty addendum

## Solara+ Extended Protection Plan

Customers may purchase Solara+ Protection at checkout or within 90 days of purchase for extended coverage:

- Extends standard warranty by up to 3 additional years
- Adds coverage for one accidental damage incident per covered item (subject to a $25–$75 service fee depending on product category)
- Includes priority support queue and next-business-day replacement shipping for verified claims on eligible smart home devices

Solara+ pricing and specific terms are shown at checkout and vary by product category and plan length.

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. Locate your order number or product serial number.
2. Visit **My Orders > Request Warranty Service**, or contact support@solarahome.com.
3. Describe the issue; our team may ask you to complete basic troubleshooting steps (see [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/) guides) to rule out a non-hardware cause.
4. If a defect is confirmed, Solara will, at its discretion:
   - Repair the item using new or refurbished parts, **or**
   - Replace the item with the same or a comparable model, **or**
   - Refund the item's depreciated value if repair/replacement is unavailable.
5. For items requiring return, a prepaid shipping label is provided at no cost.

## Geographic Coverage

Standard warranty applies to products purchased and used within the country of original sale (United States or Canada). Warranty service for products taken outside the country of purchase may be limited; contact Support before traveling internationally with a device you may need serviced.
