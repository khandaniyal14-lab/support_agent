# Refund Policy

**Solara Home Electronics**
Last updated: March 1, 2026

## Overview

At Solara Home Electronics ("Solara," "we," "us," or "our"), we want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains how and when refunds are issued for orders placed through www.solarahome.com, the Solara mobile app, or our authorized retail partners.

## Eligibility for a Refund

You may be eligible for a refund if:

- You return an eligible item within the applicable return window (see our [Return Policy](return-policy.md)).
- Your order arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect.
- Your order was cancelled before it shipped.
- A promotional price adjustment applies within 7 days of purchase (see Price Adjustments below).

Refunds are **not** issued for:

- Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance" on the product page.
- Gift cards and store credit purchases.
- Digital products (firmware licenses, extended warranty add-ons) once activated.
- Shipping charges, except when the return is due to our error (damaged, defective, or mis-shipped item).

## Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout:

| Payment Method | Refund Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Credit/debit card | 5–10 business days after we receive and inspect the item |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days |
| Solara Store Credit | Instant, upon approval |
| Financing (Solara Pay Later via Affirm) | 7–14 business days; your financing plan will be adjusted or closed accordingly |

If the original payment method is no longer valid (e.g., expired card), the refund will be issued as Solara Store Credit to the email address on file.

## Refund Process

1. Initiate a return through **My Orders > Return or Replace Item** on your account, or contact Customer Support at support@solarahome.com.
2. Ship the item back using the prepaid return label provided (for eligible returns) or your own carrier.
3. Once our warehouse receives and inspects the item (typically 2–4 business days after delivery to our facility), we process the refund.
4. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been issued.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following cases:

- Item is returned without original packaging, accessories, or manuals (up to 20% restocking deduction).
- Item shows signs of use beyond inspection/testing (assessed case-by-case by our Returns Quality team).
- Bundle purchases where only part of the bundle is returned; the refund reflects the standalone price of the returned component, not a pro-rated bundle price.

## Price Adjustments

If a product you purchased drops in price within 7 calendar days of your order (excluding flash sales, doorbuster events, and bundle-only pricing), contact Support with your order number and we will refund the difference to your original payment method as store credit.

## Late or Missing Refunds

If more than 10 business days have passed since you received your refund confirmation email and funds have not appeared:

1. Check with your bank or card issuer — processing times vary.
2. Contact your card issuer, as it may take additional time for a refund to post officially.
3. If you've done both and still have not received your refund, contact us at support@solarahome.com or 1-888-765-6272.

## Order Cancellations Before Shipment

Orders cancelled before they leave our fulfillment center are refunded in full within 3–5 business days, with no restocking fee.
