# FAQ: Shipping & Delivery

**Solara Home Electronics**

**Q: What shipping options do you offer?**
A:

| Method | Cost | Estimated Transit Time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | Free on orders $35+ (otherwise $5.99) | 4–6 business days |
| Expedited Shipping | $12.99 | 2–3 business days |
| Next-Day Shipping | $24.99 | 1 business day (order by 2 PM ET) |
| Freight Delivery (large appliances) | Calculated at checkout | 7–14 business days |

**Q: How long does it take to process my order before it ships?**
A: Most orders are picked, packed, and handed to the carrier within 1 business day. Custom/built-to-order items (e.g., custom-panel refrigerators) take 2–4 weeks to manufacture before shipping.

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves our warehouse. You can also track any order from **My Orders > Track Package**.

**Q: My tracking says "delivered" but I don't have my package. What do I do?**
A: First, check around your delivery location (porch, mailroom, with a neighbor) and confirm the shipping address on your order. Carriers sometimes mark packages delivered up to 24 hours before physical arrival. If it hasn't turned up after 48 hours, contact support@solarahome.com with your order number — we'll open a carrier investigation and, if warranted, send a replacement or refund.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: We currently ship to the United States, Canada, and select EU countries via our international partner site. Availability, pricing, and delivery times vary by destination and are shown at checkout. Import duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient unless "Duties Paid" is shown at checkout.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: Address changes are only possible before an order enters processing (typically within 60 minutes of ordering). Contact Support immediately at support@solarahome.com — we cannot guarantee a change once fulfillment has started.

**Q: Do you deliver and install large appliances?**
A: Yes. Solara Chill and Solara Cook products include an option for white-glove delivery and basic installation, schedulable at checkout or afterward via **My Orders > Schedule Installation**. Haul-away of your old appliance is available for an additional $30.

**Q: What if my item arrives damaged?**
A: Refuse delivery if the damage is visible on the box, or contact support@solarahome.com within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage and packaging. We'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund — no return of the damaged item is required in most cases.

**Q: Can I ship to a PO Box or APO/FPO address?**
A: Standard-size items (under 5 lbs) can ship to PO Boxes and APO/FPO addresses via USPS. Large items and freight-delivered appliances require a physical street address.

**Q: Do you offer same-day delivery?**
A: Same-day delivery is available in select metro areas (currently New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, and Austin) for eligible in-stock items ordered before 11 AM local time, shown as an option at checkout when available in your area.
