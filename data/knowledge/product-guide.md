# Product Guide

**Solara Home Electronics — Product Line Overview**
Last updated: March 1, 2026

Solara Home Electronics designs and sells smart home devices, home audio, floor care, and major appliances. Below is an overview of our current product lines.

---

## Solara Hub — Smart Home Hub

**Models:** Solara Hub Mini ($49.99), Solara Hub Pro ($89.99)

The Solara Hub is the central controller for your smart home, connecting Solara and select third-party devices over Wi-Fi, Thread, and Zigbee.

- **Solara Hub Mini:** Wi-Fi + Bluetooth, supports up to 30 connected devices, compact puck design.
- **Solara Hub Pro:** Adds Thread/Zigbee radios, local voice processing, supports up to 150 devices, built-in Matter controller.
- Setup: Download the Solara Home app, plug in the hub, and follow the in-app pairing wizard (typically under 5 minutes).
- Compatible with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple Home via Matter.
- Warranty: 2 years standard (see [Warranty Policy](../policies/warranty-policy.md)).

## Solara Glow — Smart Lighting

**Models:** Glow Bulb A19 ($14.99, single / $39.99 4-pack), Glow Strip ($29.99), Glow Panel ($59.99)

- Tunable white and full RGB color options (Glow Bulb Color and Glow Strip).
- Controlled via Solara Home app, voice assistants, or the physical Solara Wall Switch ($24.99).
- Supports schedules, geofencing (auto-on when you arrive home), and adaptive lighting based on time of day.
- Glow Bulb rated for 25,000 hours (~17 years at 4 hrs/day average use).
- Warranty: 2 years standard.

**Models:** Sweep 200 ($299), Sweep 400 ($449), Sweep 600 Self-Empty ($699)

| Feature | Sweep 200 | Sweep 400 | Sweep 600 Self-Empty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suction power | 2,200 Pa | 3,000 Pa | 4,000 Pa |
| Mapping | Gyroscope | LiDAR | LiDAR + obstacle avoidance camera |

## Solara Beam — Audio

**Models:** Beam Mini Speaker ($39.99), Beam Soundbar ($199.99), Beam Soundbar Pro + Subwoofer ($349.99)

- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.2 connectivity; multi-room audio grouping via the Solara Home app.

## Solara Chill — Refrigeration

**Models:** Chill Counter-Depth 22 cu ft ($1,899), Chill French Door 26 cu ft ($2,299), Chill Smart+ (with internal camera & touchscreen, 27 cu ft, $2,899)

## Solara Cook — Ranges & Ovens
