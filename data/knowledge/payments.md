# FAQ: Payments

**Solara Home Electronics**

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Solara Gift Cards/Store Credit, and Solara Pay Later (installment financing via Affirm). See our full [Payment Policy](../policies/payment-policy.md) for details.

**Q: Why was my card declined?**
A: The most common causes are insufficient funds, an incorrect billing address, an expired card, or your bank flagging the transaction. See our [Payment Failed troubleshooting guide](../troubleshooting/payment-failed.md) for step-by-step fixes.

**Q: When will my card actually be charged?**
A: For in-stock items, you're charged when your order ships, not when you place it. Pre-orders and backorders are charged when they ship. If your order ships in multiple boxes, each shipment is billed separately for its portion of the order.

**Q: I see a charge I don't recognize from Solara. What is it?**
A: Charges appear on your statement as "SOLARA HOME ELEC" followed by a reference number. This could be a shipped order, a split shipment, or a Solara+ / consumables subscription renewal. Check **My Orders** and **Account > Subscriptions** first. If you still don't recognize it, email billing@solarahome.com with the charge amount and date.

**Q: Can I use more than one gift card on an order?**
A: Yes, you can apply multiple Solara gift cards to a single order at checkout. Gift cards cannot be combined with certain third-party promotional codes; the checkout page will notify you if a conflict applies.

**Q: Do you offer financing?**
A: Yes — Solara Pay Later, powered by Affirm, is available at checkout for orders over $50. You'll see your rate and payment schedule before completing your purchase, with no impact to your credit score for the initial soft check.

**Q: Is it safe to enter my card details on your site?**
A: Yes. Solara is PCI-DSS compliant, and card payments are processed through certified partners (Stripe and PayPal). We do not store your full card number on our servers.

**Q: Can I change my payment method after placing an order?**
A: Only if the order hasn't shipped yet. Contact Support at support@solarahome.com with your order number as soon as possible — we cannot guarantee changes once an order enters processing.

**Q: How do I get a copy of my invoice/receipt?**
A: Log in to **My Orders**, select the order, and click **View Invoice** to download a PDF copy. Guest checkout customers can request a copy by emailing support@solarahome.com with the order number and billing email.

**Q: Do you charge sales tax?**
A: Yes, sales tax is calculated based on your shipping address in accordance with applicable state and local law. Verified tax-exempt organizations can submit documentation to tax-exempt@solarahome.com.
