# FAQ: Refunds

**Solara Home Electronics**

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
A: Once we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds typically post within 5–10 business days for cards, 3–5 business days for PayPal, and instantly for store credit. See our [Refund Policy](../policies/refund-policy.md) for full details by payment method.

**Q: I returned my item — why haven't I gotten a refund yet?**
A: First check your return's status in **My Orders > Track Return** to confirm it's been received at our warehouse. Inspection typically takes 2–4 business days after arrival. If it's been received and more than 10 business days have passed without a refund, contact support@solarahome.com with your order number.

**Q: Will I get my full amount back?**
A: You'll receive a full refund for items returned in original, unused condition with all packaging and accessories. Missing accessories/packaging or signs of use beyond inspection may result in a partial refund (restocking deduction of up to 20%). Original shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error.

**Q: Can I get a refund without returning the item?**
A: In most cases, no — the item must be returned first. Exceptions include confirmed damaged/defective items where we waive the return requirement, and orders cancelled before shipment.

**Q: Can I get cash instead of store credit?**
A: If you paid by card or PayPal, your refund goes back to that method, not cash or store credit, unless the original method is no longer valid. Store credit and gift card purchases are refunded as store credit only.

**Q: I received the wrong item. What are my options?**
A: You can request a free replacement of the correct item or a full refund, including original shipping — no restocking fee applies. Contact support@solarahome.com with your order number and, if possible, a photo of what you received.

**Q: My refund amount looks lower than expected. Why?**
A: This is usually due to a restocking deduction (missing packaging/accessories), a non-refundable $6.99 return label fee (for non-defect returns using our label), or partial refund of a bundle where only some items were returned. Your refund confirmation email itemizes any deductions.

**Q: Can I exchange instead of getting a refund?**
A: Yes — select "Exchange" instead of "Refund" when starting your return in **My Orders**. We ship your replacement as soon as your returned item is scanned by the carrier, rather than waiting for warehouse receipt (subject to stock availability).

**Q: I cancelled a subscription — will I get a refund for the remainder of the period?**
A: Solara+ and consumables subscriptions remain active through the end of the current billing period after cancellation, and we do not issue partial refunds for unused time, except where required by law.
